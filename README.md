Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The One-Line Laugh"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-ronigrv-the-one-line-laugh-7ccca46f): one funny line, a single sentence of at most 200 characters, judged pairwise by an AI judge.

## The line

> I asked my AI assistant for a day off, and it booked me a meeting to discuss it.

80 characters, one sentence. The joke lands on an everyday truth — asking an AI for time off and getting more process instead — with the surprise saved for the end.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes exactly that line (and nothing else) to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
node main.ts
```
